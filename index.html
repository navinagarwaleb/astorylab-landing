The Story Behind the Bookshelf

A Letter from our Founders

“

Founded by A.K. and Navii, a Canadian couple raising the question: where are the bedtime stories that look like our families? We grew up on tales of Krishna, Hanuman, and Ganesha, but couldn't find books that made today's kids feel that same wonder. So we built A Story Lab. The bookshelf we wish every Indian kid growing up far from home had, where their name and face belong inside the stories of their heritage.

— A.K. & Navii
Founders, A Story Lab

Joy multiplies when it's shared.

Gannu & Rohan, The Modak Magic

Sharing makes everything sweeter.

A festival morning, an empty tray of modaks, and one very happy baby Ganesha (Gannu) with sugar on his trunk. The Bookshelf Standard, Reimagined

We don't make generic cartoon booklets. We create museum-grade, heritage-first keepsake pieces.

Pillar 01

Your Child as the True Hero

Every single page features your child's authentic likeness and name integrated flawlessly into the narrative. They don't just read about Hanuman or Krishna; they stand side-by-side, sharing adventures and building memories.

Pillar 02

Cinematic Pixar-Quality Art

No template character swaps or copy-paste hair assets. Each page is illustrated by true artists with cinema-grade warm key lighting, soft focal depths, and rich textures. This is custom art made to hold and treasure for a lifetime.

Pillar 03

Heritage Stories, Done Right

Deities like Baby Krishna (Kanha), Ganesha (Gannu), and Hanuman (Bajju) are illustrated with the deepest reverence, matching generations of traditional stories. We customize your child, never the sacred deities. Cultural reverence always comes first.
